## Root causes and repairs

1. **Keywords beat the organisation's own description.** The legacy keyword
   table matched words in the brand name ("Baker" McKenzie → bakery, 90), in an
   English heading ("Treats for every *occasion*" → Dutch "occasions" = used
   cars, Heathrow → automotive, 90) and in a project heading ("museum", MVRDV →
   events, 95). Repairs: a title segment equal to the domain/site name only
   anchors a keyword with support in description/H1; a keyword decision anchored
   only in a heading is probable (74), not confirmed; the homepage's own
   declaration (two independent fields, or its self-description sentence) of a
   specific activity outranks keyword candidates and refines a broader identity
   of the same activity (airport within transport, cybersecurity within
   software). Homepage-only = 76 (probable); own pages corroborate it.
2. **A shop overruled the sector.** A commerce plugin (WooCommerce on a
   restaurant), donation amounts, menu prices or rental rates without any
   purchase element made a site "ecommerce" at 88–92; commerce found only on a
   shop sub-page did the same. Repairs: a shop needs a purchase element on its
   own page (cart, add-to-cart, checkout, Product schema) or a self-description
   backed by commerce evidence; an established identity stays primary unless the
   site presents itself as a shop; commerce only on sub-pages/technology is a
   sales channel and own pages decide. "Official store" is a shop; "gym clothes"
   is a product, not a gym; an energy supplier needs supply/tariff/production
   wording (an energy monitor is not a supplier).
3. **Big.dk: no answer after 300 s.** Sample-page audits (fetch, redirects,
   browser recovery, render queue) and the product second hop had no overall
   deadline. Repairs: hard stop at 270 s with `SCAN_TIMEOUT` (not charged,
   nothing stored), per-page hard stop for samples, the second hop only with time
   left.
4. **New sectors.** Declarations for waste services (Dutch/French/German
   wording), agricultural cooperatives, architecture studios, restaurants (also
   without the word "restaurant": biljartcafé + dishes), car rental; Dutch/French/
   German/Spanish/Italian self-description sentences; a product or audience named
   after a market ("restaurant software", "insurance for architects") never
   declares it. A homepage title naming the activity anchors two agreeing own pages.
5. **Remondis** was scored 59 on a "Challenge Validation" page: now no report.

## Software products are not a shop (branch fix/qa-software-product-commerce-20261024)

Live QA scan 2627bab0 (sophos.com, QA b8a35f0): sector correct (cybersecurity 90),
but "webshop: niet te bevestigen" with webshop as a secondary activity; 11 retail
checks (price, stock, variants, Merchant Center) stayed unconfirmed and pulled
evidence coverage to 74%. Cause: a sampled page with Product schema holding only a
name counted as verified product commerce (`confirmedRetailPage`,
`verifiedRepresentativeProductDetail`, `representativeRetailProduct`). Software
vendors and manufacturers publish such schema. Repair: product commerce needs an
offer (price + currency, stock status, a visible price or a buy action),
`hasProductOffer` in lib/commerce-applicability.ts.

Before → after (`run-commerce-channel.mjs`, `secuvendor.test`): unconfirmed +
secondary ecommerce → absent; shops with offers (`fietsenwinkel`, `allstep`,
`sharkfit`, `modehuis`) stay confirmed. 144 replay unchanged (79/11/2/1, no site
changes); 611 tests pass.

Browser cause seen live: `RENDER_NETWORK: net::ERR_HTTP2_PROTOCOL_ERROR` (Sophos).
Often a CDN bot manager refusing the headless browser; an HTTP/1.1 fallback could
circumvent that protection and is therefore not added without owner decision.
